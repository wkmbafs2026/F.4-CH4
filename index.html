📇 一、核心觀念：六大原始分錄簿 Six Books of Original Entry

原始分錄簿是企業首次記錄交易事項的帳簿。先按性質分類記入對應分錄簿，再過帳至分類帳。
Books of original entry record transactions for the first time by nature, before posting to ledgers.

🧾 交易折扣兩大鐵律 Two Iron Rules

❌ 不記錄 / Never recorded

交易折扣 不會出現在任何會計紀錄或帳戶中。
Trade discount is not shown in any accounting records or ledger accounts.

✅ 只記淨額 / Net only

銷貨簿與購貨簿只記 扣除折扣後淨額。
Sales & purchases journals record net amount after discount only.

📚 二、三大分類帳 + 帳戶類別 Three Ledgers + Account Types

勾選你已掌握的觀念，進度條會更新。
Tick what you have mastered to update progress.
👤 人名帳戶 Personal accounts
與企業交易的個人/公司：銷貨債務人（顧客） vs 購貨債權人（供應商）。
Trade debtors vs trade creditors.
🏠 非人名帳戶 → 實帳戶 Real accounts（會帶到下年 / carried forward）
資產、負債、資本。如：應收帳款、存貨、機器。
Assets, liabilities, capital. e.g. receivables, inventory, machinery.
💨 非人名帳戶 → 名義帳戶 Nominal（年終轉走 / transferred at year-end）
收益、費用、提用。如：租金費用、銷貨、購貨。轉至損益帳。
Revenues, expenses, drawings. e.g. rent expense. To Profit and Loss Account.

🧾 借項 vs 貸項通知單四向對照 Debit vs Credit Notes (v8 補充)

Ask "who issues it": we return to supplier → we issue a debit note; customer returns to us → we issue a credit note.

⚠️ Trap 1: debit note FROM a customer = returns inwards, never returns outwards.
⚠️ Trap 2: credit note FROM a supplier = returns outwards; debit the supplier account.

⚠️ 概念常錯區 Traps (v4)

錯處 1：非貨品銷售當銷貨 / Non-goods as Sales
銷貨 = 轉售貨品。舊電腦現金賣→現金簿；賒賣→普通日記簿（貸辦公室設備）。
Sales = goods for resale. Old computer: cash → cash book; credit → general journal.
錯處 2：現銷/現購入銷貨/購貨簿
銷貨簿只記賒銷。陳先生現銷 $700 → 現金簿。
Sales journal = credit only. Cash $700 → cash book.
錯處 3：自用設備入購貨簿
購貨簿只記轉售貨品。賒購影印機 $650 → 普通日記簿。
Purchases journal = resale goods. Photocopier → general journal.
錯處 4：貸項/借項通知單混淆
顧客退貨→賣方發貸項；退貨給供應商→買方發借項。
Customer returns → credit note; to supplier → debit note.
